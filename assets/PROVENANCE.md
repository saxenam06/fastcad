## The reference library

These are **not** inputs, and no run reads them. They live in [`../reference/`](../reference/README.md):
- the earlier CAD exports;
- 286 drawings of neighbouring parts;
- the 8 NREL reports;
- the rib-free housing;
- `tech-data/grc_techdata.yaml` and `interfaces.yaml`, written by this project from the reports, drawings and CAD.

Those two YAML files were briefly placed under `assets/` and moved out on 2026-09-16: most of their content is extractable from the artifacts, so the code must extract it, and the rest must be confirmed by an engineer rather than assumed. They may be used to *propose* values at sign-off, with their source shown.

Rebuild the library with `python scripts/make_reference.py`.
